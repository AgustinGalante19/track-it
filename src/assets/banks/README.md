# Bank and wallet logos

Twenty-one SVGs downloaded from the public **Bancos y Billeteras** catalog at
[Fintech Icons Argentina](https://icons.com.ar/) on 2026-10-14. See `sources.json`
for names and catalog IDs. The site provides Download and Copy SVG actions;
its [source repository](https://github.com/sgalanb/fintech-icons-argentina)
documents the collection. No general open-source license for the brand artwork
is stated there. The site's notice says logos and trademarks belong to their
respective owners; these assets identify the user's financial institutions and
do not imply endorsement.

SVGs are rendered as local images, preserving their original colors. There is no
runtime dependency on the catalog's internal BaseHub service or API credentials.
Unknown names, cash accounts and failed images use Lucide icons for account type.
The account's custom color continues to control its card, without tinting the logo.

ARQ uses its current official logo from https://www.arqfinance.com/ (2026-10-14),
including recognition of the former DolarApp name. Its trademark remains with ARQ.
